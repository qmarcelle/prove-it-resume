01 · PROBLEM What this lens is for.

The general surface proves platform depth so well that it hides a product engineer with a decade of customer-facing production history.

An evaluator hiring a product engineer reads the current page and sees infrastructure. That reading is accurate and incomplete: it drops years of shipping software that members, brokers and employers used, and it drops the leadership step that followed. The lens does not add claims. It reorders the same evidence so the first minute answers product engineer, senior, AI-native, and already reasoning about agent platforms, then lets architecture depth arrive as depth rather than as the headline. Second problem, smaller and real: the evidence grammar has to survive a dark environment without turning into a terminal.
